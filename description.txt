PasteFilter — Redact & Randomize Sensitive Data

Stop pasting secrets by accident. PasteFilter watches what you paste into web
pages — AI chat boxes, forms, support tickets, dashboards — and catches API
keys, passwords, and personal data before they land. Block it, mask it, or swap
in realistic-but-useless fake data. Everything runs on your device. No accounts,
no tracking, nothing ever leaves your browser.

── Four ways to react ──
• Off — stays out of the way.
• Warn — a dialog shows what was found and lets you choose what to paste.
• Mask — replaces each secret with a [[PLACEHOLDER]] automatically.
• Randomize — swaps each secret for realistic fake data, so the text still
  looks and parses like the real thing but every value is fictional.

── What it detects ──
• Secrets: private keys, JWTs, Bearer tokens, AWS / GitHub / Google / Slack /
  SendGrid / Stripe keys, database connection strings, and .env credentials.
• Personal data: emails, phone numbers, credit cards, IBANs, IP addresses,
  and EU VAT numbers.
Structured values are checksum-validated (Luhn for cards, mod-97 for IBANs),
so everyday text like order numbers or version strings is left alone.

── Private by design ──
• No network requests — detection and replacement happen locally.
• No analytics, no telemetry, no cookies, no fingerprinting.
• No sign-in. Settings and a local counter stay on your device only.

── Handy touches ──
• Toolbar popup with a live "try it" preview and one-click pause per site.
• Right-click any field or selection to clean it on demand.
• Per-detector toggles, site allowlist, and custom allowlist patterns.
• Light and dark theme that follows your system.

PasteFilter's detection engine is adapted from the open-source PasteGuard
project (Apache-2.0), rebuilt to run entirely in your browser.
